# Safety Warnings for Solar Inverter Troubleshooting

## General Safety Rules

1. **Always disconnect AC before working on the inverter.**
2. **PV panels produce electricity when exposed to light — they cannot be "turned off".**
3. **Wait at least 5 minutes after disconnecting DC before touching internal components.**
4. **Use insulated tools and wear appropriate PPE.**
5. **Never work on electrical equipment in wet conditions.**

## Critical Danger Levels

When the knowledge base indicates a **CRITICAL** danger level:

- Mandatory safety warning MUST be included in the response
- Technician must isolate both AC and DC before any physical inspection
- High voltage may be present on DC terminals even when AC is disconnected
- Do not attempt repair unless qualified and authorized

## Major Danger Levels

When the knowledge base indicates a **MAJOR** danger level:

- Caution is required
- AC isolation is recommended before inspection
- Live voltage measurements should only be taken by qualified personnel

## Medium Danger Levels

When the knowledge base indicates a **MEDIUM** danger level:

- Standard precautions apply
- Visual inspection is generally safe
- Power cycle may resolve the issue

## Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)

| Task | Required PPE |
|------|-------------|
| Visual inspection | Safety glasses, gloves |
| Voltage measurement | Insulated gloves, safety glasses, arc-flash rated clothing |
| DC wiring work | Insulated gloves, safety glasses, non-conductive footwear |
| Live panel work | Full arc-flash suit, insulated tools, rescue plan in place |

## Emergency Procedures

1. **Electric shock**: Do not touch the victim if still in contact with power. Disconnect power first. Call emergency services.
2. **Arc flash**: Evacuate area. Do not approach until power is confirmed off. Call emergency services.
3. **Fire**: Use CO2 or dry chemical extinguisher. NEVER use water near electrical equipment. Evacuate and call fire department.
